\chapter{Objectives of the Study}
\label{ch:objectives}

The study was limited to the following three objectives:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Collect, identify, and prepare the aqueous extract of \textit{Dipsacus inermis}.
  \item Evaluate the preliminary phytochemical profile of the aqueous extract.
  \item Evaluate the antibacterial activity of the extract against clinical pathogenic bacterial strains.
\end{enumerate}
