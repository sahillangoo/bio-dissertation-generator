\chapter{Materials and Methods}\label{ch:methods}

The experimental work was carried out from March 2026 to September 2026 at the Advanced Research Laboratory, Department of Zoology, University of Kashmir, Srinagar.

\section{Materials}

\subsection{Experimental plant}

\taxa{Dipsacus inermis} Wall.\ (Caprifoliaceae) was the sole plant used. Whole aerial material was taken from plants growing in the Kashmir University Botanical Garden, University of Kashmir, Srinagar (latitude 34.12981$^{\circ}$\,N, longitude 74.83396$^{\circ}$\,E; WGS84). The stand is shown in \cref{fig:collection}. The material was authenticated at the Centre for Biodiversity and Taxonomy, Department of Botany, University of Kashmir. A herbarium voucher number was not available at the time of writing and is not invented here. The taxonomic placement used in this dissertation is given in \cref{tab:plant_taxonomy}.

\subsection{Reagents, media, and equipment}

LB agar and broth were used for maintenance and for the diffusion plates. Culture media were sterilised in an autoclave before use. Distilled water was the extraction solvent, the reconstituting vehicle, and the negative control. Phytochemical reagents followed Harborne \citep{harbone1998phytochemical}: Mayer's reagent, alkaline sodium hydroxide, ferric chloride, Salkowski reagents (chloroform and concentrated sulphuric acid, or thionyl chloride as recorded in the laboratory notebook), Fehling's A and B, and concentrated sulphuric acid for the quinone tube. Commercial gentamicin discs marked GEN 5 (\SI{5}{\micro\gram}) were the positive control. Some additional plates received a methicillin disc marked MET 5; those plates are not the source of \cref{tab:zoi}. Major equipment comprised an electronic balance, heating mantle, Soxhlet assembly, autoclave, laminar-flow cabinet, bacteriological incubator at \SI{37}{\celsius}, and a millimetre scale.

\section{Methods}

\subsection{Collection, drying, and powdering}

Aerial parts were washed in distilled water and shade-dried at room temperature (\cref{fig:dried}). The dried herb was reduced to a coarse powder in a ceramic mortar (\cref{fig:mortar}). Powder was stored in a closed container, protected from light, until extraction.

\subsection{Aqueous Soxhlet extraction and working concentrations}

A \SI{50}{\gram} portion of powder was extracted with \SI{500}{\milli\litre} of distilled water (1:10, w/v) in a Soxhlet apparatus on a heating mantle (\cref{fig:weighing}, \cref{fig:soxhlet}). Extraction was run as a continuous hot extraction. The filtrate was passed through muslin and then Whatman No.\ 1 paper and concentrated. Rotary-evaporator, vacuum, and freeze-drying settings are not specified because they were not recorded beyond concentration of the liquor. The concentrated extract corresponded to 24.13\% (w/w) of the starting powder. The concentrate was reconstituted in sterile distilled water to give two working concentrations: C1, \SI{50}{\milli\gram\per\milli\litre}; C2, \SI{100}{\milli\gram\per\milli\litre}.

\subsection{Culture maintenance and inoculum}

Isolates were grown on LB agar. Pure cultures dated 01 July 2026 are shown in \cref{fig:cultures}. A colony of each organism was inoculated into LB broth, incubated at \SI{37}{\celsius}, and adjusted by eye to a 0.5 McFarland turbidity standard \citep{clsi2024m100}. The adjusted culture was spread as a lawn on LB agar before the disc-diffusion assay.

\subsection{Antibacterial assay (disc diffusion) and zone of inhibition}

The zone of inhibition is the clear area around a disc or loaded position where bacterial growth is suppressed. Antibacterial activity was read only as that diameter on LB agar plates seeded with a lawn of each isolate \citep{balouiri2016methods,bauer1966antibiotic,clsi2024m100}. No broth dilution, MIC, or MBC assay was performed.

Two extract concentrations were tested: C1, \SI{50}{\milli\gram\per\milli\litre}; C2, \SI{100}{\milli\gram\per\milli\litre}. Distilled water was the negative control. Gentamicin GEN 5 (\SI{5}{\micro\gram} disc) was the positive control. C1, C2, and distilled water (N) were placed on the seeded plates together with a GEN 5 disc marked P (\cref{fig:assaylayout}). Plates were incubated at \SI{37}{\celsius} for \SI{24}{\hour}. Each treatment was run in triplicate.

\subsection{Measurement and statistics}

The diameter of each clear zone of inhibition was measured in millimetres with a millimetre scale, inclusive of the disc. Results are reported as mean $\pm$ standard deviation ($n = 3$). No analysis of variance was applied. CLSI breakpoints were not used to call the extract susceptible or resistant \citep{clsi2024m100}.

\subsection{Ethical considerations}

No vertebrate animals were used. An Institutional Animal Care and Use Committee (IACUC) protocol is not applicable. Plant material was collected from the Kashmir University Botanical Garden with departmental access. Bacterial isolates were laboratory stocks held in the Advanced Research Laboratory; they were not taken from patients for this study.
