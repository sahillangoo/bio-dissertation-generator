\section{Taxonomic Account and Natural History}

The western fence lizard, \taxa{Sceloporus occidentalis} Baird \& Girard, 1852, 
is an abundant phrynosomatid squamate inhabiting western North America. Within 
the family Phrynosomatidae, this taxon is closely allied with \taxa{Sceloporus graciosus}. 
Morphological variation across populations of \taxa{S. occidentalis} has led to 
the description of multiple regional subspecies, including \taxa{Sceloporus occidentalis bocourtii}.
