# IACUC Animal Ethics & Research Compliance Templates

This reference provides standardized language and checklists for vertebrate animal research compliance in academic dissertations.

---

## 1. Vertebrate Animal Care & Use Committee (IACUC) Statement

Every dissertation involving live vertebrate animals must include an explicit Institutional Animal Care and Use Committee approval statement in both the **Frontmatter Ethics Statement** and the **Materials & Methods Chapter**.

### Standard Boilerplate (Field and Laboratory Research)
```latex
\section*{Ethical Approval and Animal Welfare}
All experimental protocols, field collection procedures, and animal handling methods 
described herein were conducted in strict accordance with the recommendations in the 
\textit{Guide for the Care and Use of Laboratory Animals} of the National Institutes 
of Health and were reviewed and approved by the Institutional Animal Care and Use 
Committee (IACUC) of [University Name] under Protocol Number \textbf{\#IACUC-2024-0892-VANCE}. 
Field capture, tissue biopsying, and specimen handling protocols conformed to the 
ethical guidelines established by the American Society of Ichthyologists and 
Herpetologists (ASIH) and the Animal Behavior Society (ABS).
```

## 2. Wildlife Scientific Collection Permits

Field research on wild populations requires explicit disclosure of governing municipal, state/provincial, and federal permits.

### Standard Permit Disclosure
```latex
Specimen collection was performed under California Department of Fish and Wildlife 
Scientific Collecting Permit \textbf{SC-12849} and Nevada Department of Wildlife 
Scientific Collection Permit \textbf{38921}. Research activities in federal wilderness 
areas and national monuments were authorized under National Park Service Scientific 
Research and Collecting Permit \textbf{DEVA-2023-SCI-0042}.
```

## 3. Anesthesia, Analgesia, and Euthanasia Specifications

Detailed procedures for humane endpoint handling must specify drug names, concentrations, buffers, and routes of administration:
- **Amphibians**: Immersion in buffered tricaine methanesulfonate (MS-222, 1.0–2.0 g/L neutral buffered with sodium bicarbonate to pH 7.0–7.4).
- **Reptiles**: Intracoelomic injection of sodium pentobarbital (100 mg/kg) or inhaled isoflurane followed by physical pithing or secondary thoracic incision to ensure death prior to tissue fixation.
- **Mammals / Birds**: Inhaled isoflurane vapor followed by cervical dislocation or bilateral thoracotomy in accordance with the AVMA Guidelines for the Euthanasia of Animals.
