# Sample Dissertation Scaffolding Outline

**Title**: Evolutionary Diversification, Morphometrics, and Cytonuclear Discordance in the *Sceloporus occidentalis* Complex  
**Degree**: Ph.D. in Integrative Biology  

---

## Frontmatter
- **Title Page**: Candidate Researcher, University of California, Berkeley (2026).
- **Abstract**: 320 words highlighting molecular evidence for Pleistocene introgression and morphological divergence along elevational ecotones.
- **Ethics Statement**: IACUC Protocol #IACUC-2024-0892-VANCE; California DFW Permit SC-12849.

## Chapter 1: Introduction
1. Macroecological patterns of divergence in western North America.
2. The paradox of speciation with gene flow across elevational clines.
3. Focal system: Phrynosomatid lizards (*Sceloporus occidentalis*, *S. graciosus*).
4. Three core research hypotheses ($H_1, H_2, H_3$).

## Chapter 2: Literature Review
1. Historical biogeography of the Great Basin and Sierra Nevada.
2. Phylogenomics in non-model squamates: Sanger to RAD-seq.
3. Functional morphology of substrate adaptation (saxicolous vs. arboreal).
4. Cytonuclear discordance: Mechanisms, prevalence, and analytical detection.

## Chapter 3: Materials and Methods
1. Field sampling transects across California and Nevada (86 specimens).
2. Museum voucher accessioning at Museum of Vertebrate Zoology (MVZ).
3. Mitutoyo caliper protocol for 11 linear morphometric traits.
4. DNA extraction (Qiagen DNeasy), PCR amplification of COI and RAG1.
5. Phylogenetic reconstruction: IQ-TREE 2 (ML) and RevBayes (MCMC).
6. Morphological statistics: PCA and PGLS in R.

## Chapter 4: Results
1. Sequence alignment characteristics (COI: 658 bp; RAG1: 1,020 bp).
2. Maximum likelihood and Bayesian tree topologies.
3. Deep cytonuclear discordance identified at Owens Valley contact zone.
4. Morphological differentiation: Head flattening in saxicolous populations ($F = 18.4, p < 0.001$).

## Chapter 5: Discussion
1. Synthesis: Support for $H_1$ (ecomorphological convergence) and $H_2$ (cytonuclear introgression).
2. Evaluation of historical vicariance vs. ecological divergent selection.
3. Comparison with Smith et al. (2021) phylogenomic findings.
4. Future directions: Whole-genome resequencing and thermal physiology experiments.

## Appendices
- **Appendix A**: Specimen Voucher Catalog (MVZ accession numbers, GPS coordinates).
- **Appendix B**: Primer sequences, thermocycling profiles, master mix chemistry.
- **Appendix C**: Full MANOVA tables and morphometric correlation matrices.
